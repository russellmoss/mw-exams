## Per-task analysis

### question_generation — SWITCH (applied 2026-08-05)

The single biggest line in the budget.

| Arm | Calls | Total | $/call | Avg latency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Opus 5 (50% arm) | 500 | $560.83 | $1.122 | 51.7s |
| Sonnet 4.6 (50% arm) | 499 | $74.15 | $0.149 | 30.9s |
| Sonnet 4.6 (retries/fallbacks, no arm) | 1,438 | $206.24 | $0.143 | 26.0s |

Opus costs **7.5× more per call** and is ~20s slower. Quality (arm-stamped questions):

| Arm | Questions | Invalid | Invalid rate | Feedback events | Defects upheld | Critiques overruled |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Opus 5 | 103 | 1 | 0.97% | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| Sonnet (arm) | 114 | 5 | 4.4% | 7 | 3 | 3 |
| Sonnet (no arm) | 209 | 9 | 4.3% | 8 | 0 | 6 |

Opus does generate cleaner first drafts (~1% invalid vs ~4%), but the validity auditor + repair loop
catches Sonnet's misses, and the redraft cost is already included in Sonnet's observed numbers. At a
7.5× price gap the tradeoff strongly favors Sonnet. **Verdict: SWITCH — applied today** (code defaults
in question-engine.ts / length-check.ts / model-selector.ts, plus `model_ab_config.question_generation
= {sonnet:100}`). The dependent tasks `length-check` (9.2× gap, no quality difference) and
`stem_detail_variants` (10× gap, identical feedback profile) ride the same task key and switch with it.

## Tradeoffs & risks

1. **Sonnet's first-draft invalid rate is ~4.4× Opus's** (4.3–4.4% vs 0.97%) on question generation.
   The auditor + repair loop absorbs this today (redraft cost already in Sonnet's numbers), but if the
   validator ever misses, a broken question reaches a user. Watch `invalid_reasons` rates post-switch.
2. **Sonnet question-gen has 3 upheld feedback defects vs Opus's 0** — but on 7 vs 1 feedback events,
   this is noise-level. Watch the feedback ledger over the next few weeks.
3. **model_answer's 4-vs-0 upheld defects** is why that switch is NOT being made despite a $270/mo
   prize. If quality-sensitive, the answer is the product.
4. Latency improves on generation (~31s vs ~52s) — no tradeoff there, Sonnet is faster.
