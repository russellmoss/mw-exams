### Rule-outs

- **All-Champagne-present flights** where Champagne anchors one slot AND another wine is also a prestige traditional-method style: unlikely — the examiner wants breadth, not two prestige traditional-method wines crowding the flight.
- **Lambrusco/Asti as the sole Italian answer without considering Prosecco**: keep both alive until the mousse/tannin/colour cues resolve it — Lambrusco is usually pink/red-tinted with light tannin grip, Prosecco is pale and tannin-free.
