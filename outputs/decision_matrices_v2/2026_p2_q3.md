### IMW's likely intent
By removing variety ID as an independent hurdle (region and style marks are tied at 20 each), the examiners spend maximum marks on the harder skill: discriminating quality, style and maturity between two wines from the identical origin. The explicit "considering the likely vintage" wording in part (c) additionally rewards candidates who commit to which wine is earlier/later and justify it from structural cues rather than merely describing development in isolation. The three pairs are deliberately built around three *different* contrast mechanisms (philosophy, tier, vintage) — testing whether the candidate can diagnose which mechanism is in play per pair, not apply one template to all three.
