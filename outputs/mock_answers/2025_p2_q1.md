# Mock answer — 2025 Paper 2 Question 1

## a) Identify the grape variety (12 marks)

Cabernet Franc, or Cabernet Franc-predominant. All three wines share a violet and pencil-shavings register that shifts with ripeness: overtly leafy, capsicum-edged herbaceousness at 12.5% alcohol, softening to riper red-and-black cherry fruit at 13.5%, and a fully ripe, savoury sweet-spice profile at 14.5% — the classic move from green to dried-herb pyrazine as ripeness climbs. Fine, silky tannins and moderate-to-firm acidity run through all three, ruling out the broader, more structured tannin of Cabernet Sauvignon. Merlot was also considered for the softer examples, but the persistent graphite note and lifted violet aromatics, present even in the ripest wine, point specifically to Cabernet Franc as the lead or sole variety.

## b) Identify the origin as closely as possible (3 x 6 marks)

**Wine 1** — The pale-medium ruby colour, 12.5% alcohol and pronounced redcurrant, raspberry and leafy pyrazine indicate a cool, marginal climate — the Loire Valley. Fine, precise tannins and unoaked red-fruited purity narrow this to Chinon on the Loire's alluvial gravels rather than the firmer Bourgueil or Saumur-Champigny.

**Wine 2** — Deeper ruby colour, riper cassis and blue-plum fruit and 13.5% alcohol point to a warm New World site with cooling altitude — Mendoza. The chalky, mineral-edged finish is consistent with the calcareous soils of the Uco Valley rather than the more alluvial, fruit-forward Luján de Cuyo floor.

**Wine 3** — Full, deep ruby-purple colour, 14.5% alcohol and opulent dark cherry, cedar and garrigue-like herb character point to a warm Mediterranean origin — central Italy. Polished tannin, new-oak spice and a non-indigenous variety declared outside DOC(G) rules indicate a Tuscan IGT Super Tuscan style rather than a Chianti Classico Sangiovese wine.

## c) Discuss how climate and winemaking techniques have influenced the quality and style of the wine (3 x 15 marks)

**Wine 1** — The cool, marginal growing season — high acidity, modest ripeness — explains the lean, high-toned red-fruit style and restrained 12.5% alcohol. Hand-harvesting and destemming without crushing preserved whole-berry integrity, while native-yeast fermentation in cool stainless steel protected primary fruit and the variety's leafiness. Ageing in tank only, with no oak, keeps the finish fresh, prioritising immediacy over structure. A good quality, classic village-level Chinon — precise and honest rather than profound — comparable to a well-made Chinon AOC retailing around $18–22, for early- to mid-term drinking.

**Wine 2** — The riper, high-altitude climate — warm days, cool nights, abundant sunshine — delivers the deeper colour, riper cassis fruit and 13.5% alcohol, while chalky soils lend the mineral, tensioned finish that keeps the wine from feeling heavy. Spontaneous native-yeast fermentation adds textural complexity and terroir signature; ageing in French oak contributes integrated cedar, clove and vanilla spice without masking fruit, a moderate rather than heavy new-oak regime. Very good quality, a premium New World Cabernet Franc comparable to a top single-vineyard Uco Valley red retailing around $35–45, built to reward five or more years in bottle.

**Wine 3** — The warm, dry, even growing season explains the full body, ripe dark-fruit concentration and the flight's highest alcohol at 14.5%, with the shift from green to dried-herb pyrazine (rosemary, oregano) reflecting greater ripeness at harvest. Meticulous block-by-block micro-cru vinification with selected yeasts points to a highly controlled, quality-focused approach; ageing in French oak barriques delivers polished vanilla and cedar spice and softens tannin to a silky texture. The firm acidity and soft, ripe tannin alongside the full body hint at a Cabernet Franc-led blend rather than a pure varietal bottling, in the Super Tuscan idiom. Outstanding quality, a top-tier Tuscan IGT retailing around $70–90, comparable to leading Bordeaux-blend wines from Bolgheri, with the depth and oak integration to age a decade or more.
