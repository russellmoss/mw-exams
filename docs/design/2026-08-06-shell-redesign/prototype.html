Your answer

The common variety is Syrah. Wine 1 shows white pepper, violet and a cool savoury mid-palate with fresh acid — Northern Rhône, likely Crozes-Hermitage, very good quality with typicity and length…

Wine 3 is riper: American oak sweetness, eucalyptus lift, softer acid — Australian Shiraz, Barossa, good quality but less complexity than its weight suggests… Show full answer

Debrief

Identification 14/18 · Assessment 10/15 · Quality 7/12

Variety call: correct, well argued. Pepper + violet + savoury acid line is exactly the evidence chain examiners want for Syrah, stated before the conclusion — good funnelling discipline.

Where marks were lost: Wine 2’s origin was hedged (“Rhône or cooler New World”) — commit and justify. Quality assessments leaned on scores without the because: balance, length, intensity, complexity need to be cited as evidence, not implied.
