# **Institute of Masters of Wine — Stage 1 Assessment (S1A)**

*Practical Paper Question & Wine Compilation*

*Source: Institute of Masters of Wine examination papers*

## **How to use this document**

This file is the **Stage 1 Assessment** corpus. It is deliberately kept SEPARATE from
`MW_Practical_Papers_Compilation.md`, which holds the Stage 2 MW practical papers.

The two exams are not interchangeable:

- The **Stage 2 MW practical** is three papers of 12 wines each — Paper 1 white still, Paper 2 red
  still, Paper 3 mixed/special. Every statistical artifact in this project (the master decision
  trees, the taxonomy families, the wine-distribution-by-paper analysis, the exam-structure
  predictor) is built on that paper-1/2/3 structure.
- The **Stage 1 Assessment** is a single practical paper of 12 wines that mixes sparkling, white,
  red, sweet and fortified together, with a different question count and a different mark
  distribution. Filing it as a fourth Stage 2 paper would corrupt every paper-level distribution
  those artifacts depend on.

So S1A lives here, parses to `data/s1a/` via `scripts/parse_s1a_source.py`, and carries the wine-ID
prefix `s1a_` so it can never silently join against a Stage 2 wine ID. It is available for stem
analysis and drill material; it is excluded from the Stage 2 corpus statistics.

Theory papers are not included, matching the Stage 2 compilation's convention.

## **Paper 1**

### **Question 1   *(Wines 1–2)***

Wines 1 and 2 come from the same region and the same producer.

For both wines:

a) Identify the region. (10 marks)

b) Compare quality, clearly stating which one is of higher quality. (25 marks)

c) Compare commercial potential. (15 marks)

*Notes / Examiner intent:*

### **Question 2   *(Wines 3–4)***

Wines 3 and 4 are made from the same single grape variety and come from two different countries.

For both wines:

a) Identify the grape variety. (20 marks)

b) Comment on style and quality with reference to winemaking. (30 marks)

*Notes / Examiner intent:*

### **Question 3   *(Wines 5–7)***

Wines 5-7 are red blends from three different countries.

For each wine:

a) Identify the origin as closely as possible with reference to grape varieties. (3 x 10 marks)

b) Comment on style and quality. (3 x 15 marks)

*Notes / Examiner intent:*

### **Question 4   *(Wines 8–9)***

Wines 8 and 9 are from two different countries.

For each wine:

a) Identify the origin. (2 x 8 marks)

b) Comment on the key methods of production. (2 x 7 marks)

c) Comment on style and commercial potential. (2 x 10 marks)

*Notes / Examiner intent:*

### **Question 5   *(Wines 10–12)***

Wines 10-12 are fortified. None are from Spain or Portugal.

For each wine:

a) Identify the origin as closely as possible referring to grape variety/ies used. (3 x 9 marks)

b) Comment on the method of production. (3 x 12 marks)

c) State ABV in %. (3 x 2 marks)

d) State RS in g/l. (3 x 2 marks)
