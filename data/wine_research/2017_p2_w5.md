# Montepulciano d'Abruzzo, La Piuma, 2015

## Quick tasting profile
Intense ruby red colour; aromas of black cherry, raspberry, pepper, and vanilla. Full-bodied on the palate with soft, elegant acidity and a long finish (source: bottleofitaly.com — this tasting note is drawn from the brand's general/current product description rather than a note verified specifically for the 2015 vintage; treat as PLAUSIBLE house style rather than a confirmed 2015 tasting note).

## Technical specs
- Grape variety: 100% Montepulciano
- Producer/parent company: La Piuma is a commercial brand produced by Mondo del Vino (MGM group), an Italian wine company founded in 1991 by oenologists Alfeo Martini, Roger Gabb, and Christoph Mack; the group sources and produces wines across multiple Italian regions (Abruzzo, Tuscany, Umbria, Emilia-Romagna, Puglia, Marche) (source: bottleofitaly.com)

## Producer style
La Piuma is a value-tier, high-volume commercial label owned by the Mondo del Vino group rather than an independent single-estate producer (source: bottleofitaly.com). Mondo del Vino was founded in 1991 and operates across several Italian regions, positioning its labels (including La Piuma) as accessible, everyday-drinking wines rather than terroir-specific or limited-production bottlings. Montepulciano d'Abruzzo DOC is itself one of Italy's highest-volume DOC appellations, spanning quality tiers from supermarket-level to artisan; La Piuma sits toward the commercial/entry end of that range based on confirmed retail pricing for a comparable recent vintage.
