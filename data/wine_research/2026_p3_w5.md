# Blanc de Chardonnay Brut Nature, Chavost, NV

## Quick tasting profile
A pale, taut, zero-dosage Blanc de Blancs Champagne: white flowers, green apple and citrus on the nose with a distinct saline/chalky minerality, and a lean, high-acid, bone-dry palate with little to no fruit-sweetness to soften the cut. Retailer and specialist notes consistently frame it as an austere, minimal-intervention, "growers' cooperative" style rather than a rich, dosage-softened Grande Marque blend (source: Leon & Son Wine, xtrawine, Bernabei product pages - via Tavily research).

## Technical specs
- Grape variety: 100% Chardonnay (first-press/cuvee juice)
- Vinification: 100% stainless steel, temperature-controlled; spontaneous (indigenous-yeast) alcoholic and malolactic fermentation; no commercial yeasts, enzymes, tannins, fining or chaptalisation; not fined or filtered before tirage
- Dosage: Pas Dose / Brut Nature - explicitly stated as no added sugar at disgorgement across multiple retailer listings
- ABV / RS / TA: 12.5% / 0 g/L dosage (no producer-published lab RS figure located) / not published
- Sulphur: house philosophy is "sans sulfites ajoutes" (no added sulphur) across the Chavost range; exact SO2 figures for this specific cuvee not located
- Lees ageing: release-dependent, retailer notes cite roughly 18-24 months depending on disgorgement batch
(source: importer/retailer technical pages and specialist writeup, via Tavily research; no dedicated producer technical sheet for this cuvee was found - only one exists publicly for the separate "Blanc d'Assemblage" cuvee)

## Vintage character (NV)
As a non-vintage, multi-year blend, this cuvee is built for consistency rather than expressing a single harvest; its style is driven far more by the house's zero-dosage, no-added-sulphur, all-stainless-steel philosophy than by any single vintage's weather. *(Source needed for the specific base-vintage/reserve-wine composition of this bottling.)*

## Producer style
Champagne Chavost is a growers' cooperative (not a single domaine or a Grande Marque house) that has moved toward organic/low-intervention viticulture and a strict no-additive winemaking philosophy across its range (no chaptalisation, no fining, no added sulphur on most cuvees), positioning it within the "grower/artisan" wave of contemporary Champagne alongside other small, terroir-focused producers rather than the large negociant houses. The Blanc de Chardonnay Brut Nature is the house's most austere, mineral-driven expression, contrasting with its Blanc d'Assemblage (Chardonnay/Meunier/Pinot Noir blend), which is dosed conventionally (source: McCarthy Mitchell, Symbolic Wines, via Tavily research).
