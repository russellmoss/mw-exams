# Saumur Les Plantagenets, Cave de Saumur, 2024

## Quick tasting profile
Deep ruby, youthful colour with no bricking. Nose of blackcurrant, raspberry and red/black plum, sometimes with a flinty/mineral or savoury-herbal top note. Palate is medium-bodied, juicy and fresh with soft, elegant tannins, balanced acidity and a mineral streak on a fine-to-moderate finish — an easy, "bistrot," early-drinking style rather than a wine built for cellaring (source: cuvee-france.com; ozwinecompany.com).

## Technical specs
- Grape variety: 100% Cabernet Franc (source: ozwinecompany.com)
- Vineyard: Saumur appellation sites on clay-limestone (tuffeau) subsoil; a source cites vines of roughly 40 years for this cuvee (source: ozwinecompany.com)
- Soils: Clay-limestone/tufa limestone typical of Saumur (source: ozwinecompany.com)
- Vinification: Complete destemming, vatting with maceration reported around 15-20 days in temperature-controlled vats; a related Alliance Loire (the co-op's export consortium) note on a sibling Saumur-Champigny cuvee describes 10-day maceration and a minimum six months on lees, suggesting the co-op uses controlled vat fermentation with some lees ageing across its red range, though this is not confirmed as the exact protocol for this specific bottling (source: allianceloire.com; ozwinecompany.com)
- Elevage: Not confirmed for the base rouge; oak is not indicated on any retailer/producer material reviewed — treat as stainless/concrete/neutral vat rather than barrel-aged (Source needed for confirmation of vessel)
- ABV / RS / TA: Retailer listings disagree — 12% (The Wine Society, matches exam full_text), 12.5% (Vivino) and 13% (Viniou); dry style, RS/TA not published (Source needed)

## Producer style
Cave de Saumur is a growers' cooperative founded in the 1950s, based in extensive tufa (limestone) cellars near Saint-Cyr-en-Bourg, and is a member of the Alliance Loire cooperative export group. It promotes sustainable/organic-leaning viticulture across its grower base (water conservation, biodiversity and energy commitments cited in cooperative material) and positions Les Plantagenets as an accessible, everyday-drinking range: Cabernet Franc for red and rose, Chenin Blanc for white (source: finevines.com portfolio page; ozwinecompany.com). Retail pricing for the current UK listing (The Wine Society) sits around £18-19 per bottle for the current vintage, though earlier vintage listings and a "Cave de Saumur" line show prices as low as £8.50-8.75/bottle — indicating this sits at the entry-to-mid commercial tier typical of a large Loire cooperative rather than a premium single-estate wine (source: thewinesociety.com, two listings, with the lower figures flagged as needing reconciliation against current pricing).
