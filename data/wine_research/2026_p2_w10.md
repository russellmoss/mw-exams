# San Lorenzo, Castello di Ama, 2021

## Quick tasting profile
A deep ruby, single-vineyard Chianti Classico Gran Selezione: sour cherry, red plum, dried rose petal and a savoury, faintly balsamic edge, with a fine dusting of oak spice rather than an oaky veneer. On the palate it should show classic Sangiovese acid drive, fine but present tannin (softened slightly by the Merlot and Malvasia Nera), and a long, mineral, slightly saline finish — precise and vibrant rather than heavy or jammy.

## Technical specs
- Grape variety: 80% Sangiovese, 13% Merlot, 7% Malvasia Nera — a fixed "formula" the estate has kept consistent across recent vintages (thesortingtable.com technical sheets)
- Vineyard: Single-vineyard fruit from San Lorenzo, one of Castello di Ama's four historically named crus (alongside Bellavista, La Casuccia and Montebuoni), in Gaiole in Chianti at altitude; each variety is estate-owned and fermented separately before blending
- Soils: Galestro/alberese marl-limestone typical of Gaiole in Chianti (source needed for parcel-specific detail)
- Vinification: Hand-harvested, sorted, ambient/indigenous yeast fermentation in temperature-controlled stainless steel (30–33°C); each variety macerated separately (Sangiovese ~22–24 days, Merlot ~24–25 days, Malvasia Nera ~25–26 days) with manual pump-overs; racked to barrique for malolactic fermentation, then blended to the final ratio after MLF
- Elevage: ~10–12 months in French oak barriques (225L), approximately 22% new oak for the 2021 (recent vintages range 18–22% new), medium toast, followed by a short bottle-rest before release
- ABV / RS / TA: 13.5% / <0.5 g/L / approx. 5.0–5.6 g/L total acidity; extract approx. 30–31 g/L
- Price/positioning: Premium tier of Chianti Classico Gran Selezione — average retail ≈ US $70–75 (Wine-Searcher, Zachys, Total Wine, PJ Wine), well above standard Chianti Classico and firmly in the "grower-icon" price bracket alongside Bellavista and La Casuccia

## Vintage character (2021 Tuscany)
2021 in Chianti Classico followed a cool, relatively wet spring (with some frost risk in early April) and a warm, dry summer that pushed toward a later but even harvest; the resulting wines are generally described by the estate as fresh and perfumed, with good structure and a moderate alcohol level (13.5%), consistent with a classically balanced rather than over-ripe vintage. Castello di Ama's own notes for the 2021 San Lorenzo emphasise "perfumed" black-cherry aromatics and project a drinking window into the late 2020s, suggesting freshness and mid-term ageability rather than a blockbuster, overtly powerful style.

## Producer style
Castello di Ama, under winemaker Marco Pallanti (with Lorenza Sebasti), is one of the estate benchmarks of Gaiole in Chianti and a pioneer of single-vineyard Gran Selezione bottling — San Lorenzo, Vigneto Bellavista and Vigneto La Casuccia are three separately vinified, single-cru wines rather than blends across sites. The house style favours precision, freshness and aromatic lift over sheer power, with a deliberate move in recent vintages toward lower new-oak percentages to preserve fruit purity. The estate is certified/committed to sustainable and increasingly organic-leaning viticulture and sits at the top of the Chianti Classico quality and price pyramid alongside houses like Fontodi and Felsina.
