## Producer style
Basilio Izquierdo spent over 30 years as head winemaker at CVNE, working on the Imperial, Viña Real, and Contino labels, before launching his own project in 2007 "to produce a renewed version of the Riojas from yesteryear" (source: winetourism.com). Distinctively, he does not own vineyards himself; instead he sources old, pre-clonal-selection vine parcels from growers across Rioja and produces small-batch, artisan-style wines, including a three-wine Acodo range (blanco, rosado, tinto) (source: winetourism.com, spanishwinelover.com). The winery is based in Rioja Alavesa near Laguardia (source: spanishwinelover.com).
