# Billette Rosé, Pradel, 2015. Provence, France

## Quick tasting profile
Pale salmon-pink Cotes de Provence rosé. A trade review of a Pradel/Billette-labelled Cotes de Provence rosé blend describes "pale salmon pink colour" with "watermelon and lychee" on the nose carrying through to the palate (source: winestarsapp.com review, rated 92 points). This matches the expected commercial-tier Provence style: pale colour, light-medium body, dry, fresh red-fruit/melon character, no oak.

## Producer identity — important caveat
"Pradel" and "Billette" are related but not identical brands. Both are owned by Listel SAS (part of a large French wine group headquartered at Chateau de Villeroy, Sete). Billette is Listel's high-volume, mass-market Cotes de Provence rosé brand (reported at roughly 3.3 million bottles sold). Pradel is a separate, older Provence brand (created 1952) that Listel relaunched at a premium positioning, distinct from and above Billette in price tier (source: rayon-boissons.com trade article on the Pradel relaunch). Some retail listings combine the names ("Pradel Rose Blend Billette"), which appears to reflect shared corporate ownership/label history rather than two independent estates. The exact relationship for the specific "Billette Rosé, Pradel, 2015" label in this exam slot — i.e. whether it was a Billette-brand wine using a "Pradel" vineyard/address, or a Pradel-brand wine referencing Billette — could not be fully disambiguated from the sources found. ## Vintage character (2015 Provence)
2015 combined a wet winter, a warm dry spring and a hot but largely dry summer, producing early phenology, generally reduced yields, clean fruit and high overall quality across Provence; the regional syndicate (CIVP) recorded roughly 154 million bottles of rosé that year. Producers used the vintage's conditions for aromatically fresh, pale rosés with good concentration, working with conservative cellar choices (short maceration, cool fermentation, hand-picking in top estates) to preserve freshness despite the warm season (source: Vins de Provence 2015 vintage report PDF).

## Producer style
Billette is Listel's flagship, high-volume Cotes de Provence rosé — a mainstream supermarket-tier brand with multi-million-bottle distribution and export emphasis into Africa, Russia and Asia. Pradel, revived by the same parent company, was positioned in 2021+ marketing as a step above Billette in price (Pradel Elite and Pradel Imperial tiers), explicitly to escape the price competition affecting Billette (source: rayon-boissons.com). Both sit at the commercial, large-scale end of Provence rosé production rather than boutique/estate level.

Used in 2017 Paper 3 Q1 as one of three rosés from different countries (with Cloudy Bay Pelorus Rosé, w3, and a third), testing origin identification by style, winemaking technique, and commercial/market positioning. As a large-volume, commercial Cotes de Provence rosé, it represents the mainstream, direct-press, dry, pale-style benchmark against which the sparkling New Zealand and (per the question's third wine) other rosé styles are contrasted.
