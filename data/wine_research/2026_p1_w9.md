# Riesling, Pewsey Vale, 2024. Eden Valley, South Australia

## Quick tasting profile
Pale straw with delicate green hues; a lifted, precise nose of lime, lemon, white flowers and fine dried herbs. The palate is featherweight and taut — crisp, laser-like acidity, chalky/talc minerality and a snappy, dry finish, with no oak influence (source: multiple retail/critic listings, e.g. Nicks Wine Merchants, Wine.com). At 11.5% ABV this is the lightest-bodied, most overtly primary-fruited of the flight, sitting at the classic austere end of Eden Valley Riesling style.

## Technical specs
- Grape variety: 100% Riesling
- Vineyard: Pewsey Vale's Eden Valley estate, elevation approximately 490 m, on shallow sandy soils with quartz and schist gravel on gentle north-facing slopes — conditions associated with Eden Valley's characteristic lime, floral and mineral expression (source: estate/retail profile pages)
- Soils: Shallow sandy loam over quartz/schist gravel (source: Pewsey Vale/retail vineyard descriptions)
- Vinification: Stainless-steel fermentation, unoaked, screw-cap closure (source: retail listings, e.g. Wine.com, Nicks)
- Elevage: Brief stainless-steel maturation before bottling, no lees-driven complexity noted for this bottling (contrast with the estate's old-vine "1961 Block" cuvée, which is wild-fermented and lees-aged) (source: Pewsey Vale product pages)
- ABV / RS / TA: 11.5% (multiple retail/critic sources; one merchant listing gives 12.5% for what may be the same label — flagged as a discrepancy) / RS not published for this specific bottling — the estate's related 1961 Block Riesling (12% ABV) shows 0.9 g/L RS, pH 3.02, TA 6.81 g/L, offering a directional (not identical) reference for house dry style / TA: Source needed for this exact bottling

## Vintage character (2024 Eden Valley)
No official Bureau of Meteorology or Wine Australia regional 2024 Eden Valley report was found in available sources ("Source needed" for temperature, rainfall and harvest-timing data). Reviewer and merchant commentary consistently describes the 2024 Pewsey Vale Riesling as bright, lime-driven, floral and precise, with some noting a marginally gentler acidity profile than other Eden Valley vintages while retaining the subregion's hallmark precision (source: Wine-Searcher, Wine-pages tasting notes).

## Producer style
Pewsey Vale is one of Eden Valley's foundational Riesling sites — vines were first planted there in 1847, and the property is often credited as the first vineyard to plant Riesling in Eden Valley; it was replanted and revived in the 1960s by the Hill-Smith family (owners of Yalumba), and current winemaking is associated with Louisa Rose. The estate range runs from the everyday Eden Valley Dry/Estate Riesling (this wine) through to the old-vine 1961 Block single-block bottling, both unoaked, screw-capped and built around Eden Valley's hallmark bone-dry, high-acid, long-lived house style (source: Pewsey Vale/Nicks/Wine-Searcher profiles).

## Why it's in this exam
Wine 9 of Q2's three-wine Riesling flight across three countries. As the youngest wine in the trio (2 years old at the 2026 sitting, vs. 6 for the Austrian and 9 for the Alsace wine), it is the clear "primary fruit, no development" anchor of the maturity question — screw-cap freshness, pure lime/citrus, minimal or no petrol, and Eden Valley's characteristically lower alcohol (11.5%) versus Wachau/Alsace should help candidates place it as New World and youngest, testing recognition that Australian Riesling's austere, high-acid dry style is a deliberate stylistic choice rather than under-ripeness.
