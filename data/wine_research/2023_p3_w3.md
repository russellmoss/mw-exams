# Brut Blanc de Blancs, Nyetimber, 2014

## Quick tasting profile
Pale straw to light lemon-gold with a fine, persistent mousse. Blind, this should read as serious, extended-lees-aged traditional-method Chardonnay: baked lemon, white peach and blossom on the nose, brioche/toast and almond from long autolysis, a saline, mineral seam and pronounced, well-integrated acidity, building to a long, structured finish with real textural weight — closer in seriousness to a mature vintage Champagne Blanc de Blancs than to an entry-level Brut (source: https://nyetimber.com/product/blanc-de-blancs; corroborated by https://iwsc.net/results/detail/135956/blanc-de-blancs-brut-2014 and https://jeroboamstrade.co.uk/product/2014-nyetimber-premiere-cuvee-blanc-de-blancs-nyetimber-vineyard-w-sussex).

## Technical specs
- Grape variety: 100% Chardonnay (source: https://nyetimber.com/product/blanc-de-blancs; https://iwsc.net/results/detail/135956/blanc-de-blancs-brut-2014)
- Vineyard: Estate fruit from south-facing sites in the lee of the South Downs, West Sussex, on chalk and greensand soils; Nyetimber's chalk plantings are relatively young (first chalk harvest 2013), with the majority of the estate historically on greensand (source: https://nyetimber.com/product/blanc-de-blancs; https://worldoffinewine.com/news-features/chalk-talk-the-geology-of-english-sparkling-wine-4738831)
- Soils: Cretaceous chalk (South Downs Chalk Group) interleaved with pockets of Upper Greensand; both are free-draining and calcareous-to-acidic in character, broadly analogous to but pedologically distinct from Champagne's deeper chalk reservoirs (source: https://worldoffinewine.com/news-features/chalk-talk-the-geology-of-english-sparkling-wine-4738831)
- Vinification: Traditional method (méthode champenoise-style secondary fermentation in bottle); hand-picked, parcel-selected fruit vinified separately before blending (source: https://nyetimber.com/our-story; https://nyetimber.com/product/blanc-de-blancs)
- Elevage: Extended lees ageing — Nyetimber's own material states "over four years" on lees, with an independent tasting record citing five years for this specific 2014 bottling; bottled March 2015, disgorged March 2020, released to market around 2021 (source: https://nyetimber.com/product/blanc-de-blancs; disgorgement/release chronology via https://jeroboamstrade.co.uk/product/2014-nyetimber-premiere-cuvee-blanc-de-blancs-nyetimber-vineyard-w-sussex)
- ABV / RS / TA: 12.0% / Brut-level dosage, no single confirmed g/L figure published specifically for the 2014 release (producer material shows a range across vintages of roughly 8-12 g/L) / Source needed for a 2014-specific TA figure

## Vintage character (2014 West Sussex)
2014 is reported by Nyetimber as a warm, fine growing season that delivered an excellent, fully ripe Chardonnay harvest, allowing the estate to make a Blanc de Blancs that reflects those riper conditions rather than a lean, marginal-ripeness English vintage (source: https://falstaff.com/en/news/nyetimber-releases-blanc-de-blancs-2014). Combined with the roughly five years of lees ageing before disgorgement, the wine should show more textural generosity, riper stone-fruit character and less green austerity than earlier or more marginal English vintages, while still carrying the high, structuring acidity typical of the region's cool climate.

## Producer style
Nyetimber was founded in 1988 when Americans Stuart and Sandy Moss planted the first vines on the West Sussex estate specifically for traditional-method sparkling wine, using the three Champagne varieties; its first commercial release, the 1992 Blanc de Blancs, is widely credited as a founding moment for serious modern English sparkling wine (source: https://nyetimber.com/our-story; https://decanter.com/premium/producer-profile-nyetimber-440214). Dutch entrepreneur Eric Heerema bought the estate in 2006 and drove major vineyard expansion (multi-site holdings across Sussex, Hampshire and Kent) and a new winemaking team (Cherie Spriggs and Brad Greatrix, from 2007), pushing Nyetimber toward premium, single-site and long-aged prestige bottlings and international distribution (source: https://worldoffinewine.com/news-features/nyetimber-english-sparkling-wine). Nyetimber is consistently positioned as England's leading and most internationally recognized sparkling wine producer, with this Blanc de Blancs sitting as one of its flagship, vintage-dated, extended-lees cuvées rather than an entry-level wine (source: https://theworlds50best.com/vineyards/best-in-the-world/the-list/31-40/Nyetimber.html).

## Why it's in this exam
Wine 3 closes 2023 Paper 3 Question 1, where wines 1-4 are traditional-method sparkling wines from four different countries, none from Champagne, testing origin, grape variety/ies, quality-in-context and stated residual sugar. As the English entry, this vintage 100% Chardonnay Blanc de Blancs — with genuinely extended lees ageing on chalk/greensand fruit — is a deliberately high-bar example designed to be mistaken for grower Champagne, testing whether candidates can identify quality English sparkling wine on its own terms rather than defaulting to "Champagne" whenever a wine shows serious autolytic complexity.
