# Pelorus Rosé, Cloudy Bay, NV. Marlborough, New Zealand

## Quick tasting profile
Pale salmon hue with a fine, persistent mousse. Cloudy Bay's own tasting note describes raspberry, cranberry, wild strawberry, rosehip and marzipan on the nose, with baked quince, cranberry, brioche and soft citrus on a dry, fine-structured palate (source: Cloudy Bay tasting-note PDF). Decanter's independent review notes fresh apple, savoury biscuit-like autolysis and lemon zest (source: Decanter). The autolytic brioche/biscuit character and red-berry fruit are the key discriminators versus a still rosé.

## Technical specs
- Grape variety: Chardonnay and Pinot Noir blend; Cloudy Bay's own materials confirm the two varieties but do not publish one definitive percentage split — secondary sources conflict (Decanter/WineWiki cite roughly 70% Chardonnay/30% Pinot Noir; a New Zealand retailer's producer PDF states 80% Pinot Noir/20% Chardonnay). Exact current-vintage percentage: Source needed.
- Vineyard: Cloudy Bay's Wairau Valley estate vineyards, Marlborough
- Vinification: Whole-bunch pressing of the base fruit; primary fermentation in stainless steel with malolactic conversion, plus a small portion fermented warmer in old French oak barriques/large-format oak and a portion with wild yeast to build texture (source: Cloudy Bay site / producer PDF)
- Method: Méthode traditionnelle — tirage, secondary fermentation and a minimum of 24 months ageing on lees before disgorgement, small dosage added, cork finish (source: Cloudy Bay tasting-note PDF)
- ABV: 12.5% per Cloudy Bay's own site and PDF (note: a couple of third-party retailer listings show 13%, an unresolved minor discrepancy)
- RS/TA: Not confirmed to a specific g/L figure across the retrieved sources (producer materials describe a "soft dry finish"); style is dry sparkling. Source needed for exact dosage/RS number.

## Vintage character (NV)
Non-vintage, blended across years for house-style consistency; no single-vintage weather report applies. The minimum two years on lees is the main determinant of the wine's autolytic character rather than any one growing season (source: Cloudy Bay tasting-note PDF).

## Producer style
Cloudy Bay is Marlborough's most internationally recognised wine brand, founded in 1985 by David Hohnen and now part of Moët Hennessy (LVMH). Best known globally for its Sauvignon Blanc, the estate also produces the Pelorus range as its dedicated traditional-method sparkling line, made in both still-free NV and vintage formats, white and rosé. The brand sits at premium/luxury positioning in the on-trade and specialist retail channels (source: Cloudy Bay product pages).

## Why it's in this exam
Used in 2017 Paper 3 Q1 alongside Pradel Billette Rosé (w2) and (per slot ordering) a third rosé, testing candidates on identifying rosé production method and origin from style alone. Establishing that Pelorus Rosé is a traditional-method **sparkling** rosé, not a still one, is the critical discriminator the examiner is testing for in this flight — a candidate who reads it as a still Provence-style rosé would misjudge both winemaking technique and market positioning.
