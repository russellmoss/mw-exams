# Chablis La Butte "O", Patrick Piuze, 2021

## Quick tasting profile
Taut, high-energy, medium-bodied Chablis with green apple, lemon zest, oyster shell, sea spray, white flowers and crushed stone. Structure is saline, bone-dry and crystalline with lively, frost-vintage acidity and no discernible oak or buttery weight — a precise, terroir-transparent expression rather than a rich or reductive one (source: WineBuyerApp tasting note).

## Technical specs
- Grape variety: 100% Chardonnay
- Vineyard: A village-level parcel actually located within Butteaux but declassified/labelled as village Chablis rather than Premier Cru; introduced as a new cuvee in 2020 (source: DuckDuckGo-aggregated retailer/press summary of the cuvee's origin)

## Vintage character (2021 Chablis)
2021 was defined by catastrophic April frost (three successive hard-frost nights from April 5-6), with regional average yields around 35 hl/ha and many individual producers down to ~15 hl/ha — roughly half a normal-year crop. Despite the volume collapse, the resulting wines show classically Chablisian character: piercing precision and elegance over concentration, with middling ageing potential rather than blockbuster structure (source: web search synthesis of Burgundy trade press).

## Producer style
Patrick Piuze is a Montreal-born micro-négociant who founded his own house in Chablis in 2008 after training under Olivier Leflaive and Jean-Marie Guffens, and after winemaking experience in Australia and South Africa. He owns no vines, instead building long-term trust-based contracts with growers of old-vine parcels across Chablis, and describes himself as a "winemaker" rather than "wine grower," focused on revealing site character. His ~110,000-bottle portfolio spans four village Chablis references, eleven Premier Crus and six Grand Crus (including Les Preuses and Grenouilles), positioning him as a serious, terroir-driven specialist rather than a volume négociant (source: patrickpiuze.com biography; Sipwander and Winesaint producer profiles).

## Why it's in this exam
As wine 5 of 6 in a single-variety, five-country Chardonnay flight, this frost-reduced 2021 village Chablis is almost certainly the classic cool-climate Old World benchmark — unoaked-reading, saline and high-acid — set against the riper Italian (wine 4) and overtly oaked Californian commercial wine (wine 6) to test the candidate's ability to separate winemaking influence from origin-driven acidity and minerality.
