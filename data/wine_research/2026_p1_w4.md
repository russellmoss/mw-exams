# Chardonnay Terra Alpina, Alois Lageder, 2024

## Quick tasting profile
Pale straw-green, aromatically fresh Chardonnay showing peach, melon, citrus and floral lift with a mineral undertow rather than tropical richness. The palate is clean, precise and medium-bodied with lively acidity, no oak signature, and a dry, refreshing finish — a mountain-inflected, unoaked style rather than a rich Burgundian one.

## Technical specs
- Grape variety: 100% Chardonnay
- Vineyard: Estate and ~60 partner biodynamic/organic vineyards on rocky, sandy soils with limestone and porphyry influence, elevations roughly 230-500m in the Dolomites (source: Chambers Wines / Dalla Terra tech sheets)
- Vinification: Soft crushing/whole-cluster pressing, fermentation in temperature-controlled stainless steel (concrete used elsewhere in the estate's Chardonnay program); no clear evidence of oak use in the primary technical sheets, though a minority of retailer listings mention "a few months in oak" — this is an unresolved conflict in public documentation (source: Chambers Wines / VNTG Imports tech sheets vs. BottleOfItaly retail copy)
- Malolactic fermentation: Not explicitly stated in any consulted technical sheet — Source needed
- Elevage: ~3-4 months on fine lees before bottling, cellulose sheet filtration (source: Chambers Wines tech sheet)
- ABV / RS / TA: 12% (2024, per exam full_text; sheets for other vintages report 11.5-13%) / ~0 g/L (RS reported as 0 in one importer sheet) / ~5.7-5.9 g/L (source: Chambers Wines tech sheet)
- Yield/production: ~8,100 kg/ha, ~30,000 bottles for the referenced release lot (source: Chambers Wines tech sheet)
- Retail price: roughly US$15-20 per bottle, clustering around $17-18 (source: Wine-Searcher, Binny's, Xtrawine)

## Producer style
Alois Lageder is a sixth-generation Alto Adige family estate (founded 1823) and a pioneer of biodynamic viticulture in the region, having converted in 2004; since 2024 all ~60 partner vineyards farm organically or biodynamically, and the winery runs on solar, water and geothermal energy. The house style across its broad, 30+ variety portfolio favors freshness, precision and terroir transparency over weight or oak, with Terra Alpina positioned as an accessible, everyday-luxury entry point beneath the estate's single-vineyard and Cru bottlings (source: Alois Lageder public materials via web search synthesis).
