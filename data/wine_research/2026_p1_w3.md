# Chardonnay Prelude, Leeuwin Estate, 2024

## Quick tasting profile
Lemon-straw colour; a restrained, nutty and complex nose of citrus, pear and nectarine layered with vanillin oak spice, cedar, nougat/chestnut and a flinty edge; palate is generously fruited (nectarine, peach, persimmon) with bright acidity carrying a long, harmonious finish. Reviewers describe it as ready to drink now but capable of building over the medium term (source: Leeuwin Estate product notes; Vinous review).

## Technical specs
- Grape variety: 100% Chardonnay, including fruit from Gin Gin-clone plantings per Leeuwin's media materials (exact clone breakdown for the 2024 blend not published)
- Vineyard: sourced across multiple Margaret River vineyard blocks (Prelude is a broader, multi-vineyard regional blend, distinct from Leeuwin's single-estate flagship Art Series Chardonnay); no official parcel/harvest-date breakdown published for 2024
- Vinification: sources conflict on detail — one independent review cites French barrique fermentation with roughly 35% new oak and 10 months' maturation; a merchant technical summary instead describes fermentation split across stainless steel and seasoned (older) French oak barrels with partial malolactic fermentation and regular lees stirring (battonage); Leeuwin's own product copy states only "framed by delicate oak" without percentages. Treat exact new-oak % and vessel split as unresolved (source needed for an official technical sheet).
- Elevage: several months (approx. 8-10 months cited by independent sources) on lees in oak, with regular battonage; partial MLF in most vintages per trade description; screwcap closure
- ABV / RS / TA: reported inconsistently across sources as either 14% or 13.5% (data/wines.json states 14%) / RS not published (dry) / TA not published

## Vintage character (2024 Margaret River)
2024 was a warm, dry Margaret River season with a compressed white-grape harvest and generally lower yields; despite this, reviewers note the Prelude 2024 avoids heavy ripeness or over-extraction and instead shows restrained, balanced fruit with good acid retention — consistent with Margaret River's maritime-moderated climate tempering an otherwise warm vintage (source: merchant/marketplace vintage commentary; a dedicated official Margaret River vintage report was not located - source needed).

## Producer style
Leeuwin Estate is one of Margaret River's founding "Group of Five" wineries (est. 1974, guided early on by input from Robert Mondavi) and built its reputation on the flagship Art Series Chardonnay, regarded as a benchmark for age-worthy, powerful, barrel-fermented Australian Chardonnay. Prelude Vineyards Chardonnay is positioned as Leeuwin's accessible, earlier-drinking, multi-vineyard regional label beneath Art Series — retail price around AUD 44 (Leeuwin's own shop), with market listings ranging roughly AUD 28-72 depending on channel, placing it in a premium-but-approachable commercial tier rather than the collector-market pricing of Art Series (source: Leeuwin Estate shop; Wine-Searcher aggregation).

## Why it's in this exam
Wines 1-6 of 2026 Paper 1 Question 1 are all Chardonnay from six different countries; Leeuwin Estate's Prelude supplies the New World, warm-climate Southern Hemisphere reference point — its partial-MLF, mixed-vessel/moderate-new-oak regime and accessible commercial tier (an entry point beneath Leeuwin's famous Art Series) test candidates on distinguishing Margaret River's restrained, maritime-tempered Chardonnay style and mid-tier commercial positioning from the barrel-driven Marlborough wine and the more traditional Burgundian benchmark elsewhere in the flight.
