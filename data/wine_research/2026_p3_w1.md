# Lambrusco del Fondatore, Cleto Chiarli, 2024

## Quick tasting profile
Bright, light-to-mid ruby with a soft, low-pressure mousse of large-ish, fast-fading bubbles rather than fine Champagne-style bead — a visual tell for ancestral-method sparkling. Nose is pure and floral (violet, rose) over wild strawberry, raspberry, rhubarb and red apple, with a faint bready/yeasty lift from bottle lees. Palate is dry, high-acid, light-bodied, gently tannic on the finish (a small giveaway that it's red and skin-contact, however brief), fresh and fruit-driven rather than autolytic-dominant (source: superiore.de tech sheet; thewinecountry.com review).

## Technical specs
- Grape variety: 100% Lambrusco di Sorbara
- Vinification: manual harvest, soft pressing, ~24h light maceration at 10-12°C, primary fermentation, then bottled unfinished for **prise de mousse in the closed bottle — Metodo Ancestrale (ancestral method / "fermentato in bottiglia")**, not Charmat (source: chiarli.it wine-story page explicitly frames Fondatore around ancestral bottle fermentation, continuity with pre-industrial Lambrusco tradition before the mid-20th-century shift of the wider trade to Charmat; carsonwines.com importer tech sheet)
- Lees contact: ~6 months on lees post bottle-fermentation, contributing subtle yeast/textural lift (source: superiore.de)
- Disgorgement/dosage: sources conflict — one trade note states the wine is left non-dosé, unfiltered and undisgorged (sur lie style); a dealer tech sheet instead records light filtration and cold stabilisation before bottling. Both agree there is effectively **no dosage** (source: thewinecountry.com vs. superiore.de — flagged conflict, not resolved by producer copy)
- ABV / RS / TA: 11.5% / **3.15 g/L RS** / 9.85 g/L TA; total extract 25.61 g/L (source: superiore.de 2024 technical data)
- Malolactic, oak: none noted; stainless/glass only

## Producer style
Founded 1860, family-run for five generations (currently Mauro and Anselmo Chiarli), Cleto Chiarli is one of the oldest and most historically significant Lambrusco houses and is credited with pioneering the mushroom cork in the 1800s. The estate's broader range spans commercial Charmat-method Lambrusco through to this heritage ancestral-method bottling, which the producer markets explicitly as a link back to pre-industrial practice — i.e. Fondatore is deliberately NOT the house's standard Charmat wine. Equalitas-certified for sustainability, with organic parcels (source: cletochiarli.com/sustainability).

## Why it's in this exam
Wine 1 of a four-wine sparkling flight (Q1, Paper 3) spanning four countries; candidates must call origin, quality-in-context, and key winemaking decisions. This wine tests whether candidates can (a) recognise a red, low-pressure, ancestral-method sparkler as distinct from Prosecco-style Charmat or Champagne-style traditional method, and (b) correctly read the fine, fast bubbles/light tannin/low dosage as diagnostic of bottle-conditioned production rather than tank conditioning — a common examiner trap given how often candidates default to "Charmat = all Lambrusco."
