# Tokaji Aszú 5 Puttonyos, Disznókő, 2013

## Quick tasting profile
Deep amber-gold and intensely botrytis-scented: dried apricot, orange marmalade, honey, saffron and toasted-nut/vanilla oak notes, with a creamy, viscous mid-palate. At 156 g/L residual sugar the wine is very sweet, but a high 7.6 g/L total acidity keeps it lifted rather than cloying, with a long, concentrated finish typical of top-tier Aszú (source: https://gonzalezbyass.com/sites/www.gonzalezbyass.com/files/2025-10/2013%20DISZNO%CC%81KO%CC%8B%20TOKAJI%20ASZU%CC%81%205%20PUTTONYOS_EN.pdf).

## Technical specs
- Grape varieties (Aszú component): 75% Furmint, 15% Zéta, 10% Hárslevelű; base wine 50% Furmint / 50% Hárslevelű (source: producer technical sheet, as above)
- Vineyard: Disznókő's single contiguous ~104-hectare estate at Mezőzombor, Tokaj-Hegyalja (source: https://disznoko.hu/en/history)
- Harvest: aszú (botrytised) berries hand-picked in multiple selective passes from 23 September to 11 November 2013 — three passes through Furmint, two through Hárslevelű and Zéta (source: producer technical sheet)
- Vinification: roughly 25% of the most concentrated, shrivelled berries added directly into fermenting must; the remaining aszú berries macerated in base wine that was finishing fermentation, followed by long pressing of the macerated material (source: producer technical sheet)
- Elevage: approximately 18-20 months in 225L oak barrels (Gönci-style casks, reportedly of several vintages rather than predominantly new) in Disznókő's underground Tokaj cellar; retail summaries note maturation on fine lees with bâtonnage contributing to the creamy texture (source: producer technical sheet)
- ABV / RS / TA: 12.0% / 156 g/L / 7.6 g/L (source: producer technical sheet)
- Bottle format: 500ml; bottled June 2016 per the producer sheet (one retailer lists August 2017 — a minor, unresolved discrepancy in secondary sources)
- Critic score: Wine Advocate 96 (per retailer listing; primary review text not verified in this pass — Source needed)

**Puttonyos/labeling note:** 2013 was the first vintage under Tokaj's revised Aszú rules, which replaced the old 3-6 puttonyos count with residual-sugar thresholds (5 Puttonyos requires a minimum of 120 g/L RS, 6 Puttonyos a minimum of 150 g/L; 3- and 4-puttonyos categories were abolished). At 156 g/L, this wine's RS numerically clears even the 6-puttonyos threshold, yet it is labeled 5 Puttonyos on the producer's own sheet — evidently a producer labeling choice rather than a legal requirement to label at the higher tier (source: producer technical sheet; https://winewithseth.com/winewiki/tokaji-aszu).

## Vintage character (2013 Tokaj)
2013 is widely regarded as one of Tokaj's strongest recent Aszú vintages. A mild, wet winter recharged water reserves; a late-March frost/snow event largely spared hillside vineyards; a cool, rainy spring affected flowering slightly without cutting yields severely; then a hot, dry August was followed by a cooler, rainy September that triggered strong, sustained botrytis development running into November. This allowed extended, highly selective multi-pass picking — Disznókő reported its largest Aszú harvest in twenty years, both abundant and high in quality (source: https://gonzalezbyass.com/sites/www.gonzalezbyass.com/files/2025-10/2013%20DISZNO%CC%81KO%CC%8B%20TOKAJI%20ASZU%CC%81%205%20PUTTONYOS_EN.pdf; https://tastehungary.com/journal/understanding-tokaj).

## Producer style
Disznókő is a historic, single-estate Tokaj property dating to the 15th century, nationalized under communism and re-established as a private company in 1992 when it was acquired by AXA Millésimes — the same Bordeaux-based fine-wine investment group behind Château Pichon Baron. Since acquisition, Disznókő has been run as a large, contiguous, exclusively estate-fruit operation with modern vinification facilities and parcel-by-parcel selection married to traditional barrel ageing, positioning it as one of Tokaj's most consistent and internationally recognized benchmark Aszú producers alongside Royal Tokaji, Oremus and Szepsy (source: https://forbes.com/sites/karlsson/2016/10/25/axa-insurances-prestigious-wine-estates-in-bordeaux-portugal-and-hungary; https://disznoko.hu/en/history).
