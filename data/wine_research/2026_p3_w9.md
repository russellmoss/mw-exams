# Château Coutet, 2022. Barsac, France.

## Quick tasting profile
Bright gold, viscous. Nose is expressive: dried fruit (quince, fig), grilled/vanilla oak spice and refreshing ginger, preserved lemon and lemon-curd lift over the botrytis richness. Palate combines power and complexity with a refreshing, full, well-balanced finish — the searing 2022 heat and drought are offset by real freshness from the estate's Sauvignon Blanc component and high natural acidity (pH 3.88) (source: importer technical sheet, lanigan-edwards.com/sauternes/c_coutet22.pdf). Barsac's characteristic slightly lighter, fresher profile relative to Sauternes proper should still be evident despite the RS being very high.

## Technical specs
- Grape variety: 94% Sémillon, 5% Sauvignon Blanc, 1% Muscadelle in the 2022 bottled blend (vineyard planted to 75% Sémillon / 23% Sauvignon Blanc / 2% Muscadelle) (source: lanigan-edwards.com/sauternes/c_coutet22.pdf)
- Vineyard: 42-hectare estate (38.5 ha under vine) — the largest in Barsac; average vine age 35 years; Riparia-Gloire and 420A rootstocks at 7,500 vines/ha; gravel and red clay over a fossil-rich limestone bedrock (source: same sheet)
- Vinification: 100% barrel-fermented, 50% new French oak
- Harvest: hand-harvested in **four successive selective passes (tries)** between 26 September and 28 October 2022; house average across vintages is around six tries, reaching double figures in the most demanding years; only ~50% of the harvested grand vin fruit makes the final blend; yields were exceptionally low at 9 hl/ha, driven by botrytis-induced berry dehydration (source: same sheet)
- Elevage: 18 months in French oak barrels (50% new)
- ABV / RS / TA: 13.0% ABV per the importer's laboratory analysis (label ABV is reported elsewhere, incl. the exam full_text, as 13.5% — both figures appear on genuine trade documents and likely reflect minor market/label rounding rather than a real discrepancy) / **Residual sugar: 158 g/L** / pH 3.88 (source: lanigan-edwards.com/sauternes/c_coutet22.pdf)

## Vintage character (2022 Sauternes-Barsac)
2022 was one of the driest and hottest vintages on record in Bordeaux, with a cold winter, mild spring (interrupted by an early-April frost that did not damage this estate) and a summer of severe heat and drought that drove low yields. The turning point for botrytis was heavy rainfall at the very end of September and start of October, which triggered noble rot development just as harvest began; picking ran across four selective tries into late October to capture botrytised fruit at the right stage. The result, per the estate's own vintage note, is a concentrated, high-RS wine (158 g/L) balanced by real freshness rather than flabbiness, reflected in strong critical scores (94-96 points from Wine Spectator, Wine Advocate, Decanter, Vinous, Jane Anson and Tim Atkin MW) (source: lanigan-edwards.com/sauternes/c_coutet22.pdf).

## Producer style
Château Coutet is a Barsac Premier Cru Classé (1855), the appellation's largest and one of its oldest estates (a 13th-century tower survives on the property), owned by the Baly family (Marcel Baly, then sons Philippe and Dominique) since 1977. The estate is certified Organic Agriculture and sets aside 25% of its land for parks/gardens as a biodiversity buffer. Coutet is consistently ranked among Barsac's top two estates alongside Château Climens, and is known for a slightly lighter, fresher, more citrus-and-orchard-fruit-driven style than the richer, more opulent wines of Sauternes proper across the Ciron. Production of the grand vin runs roughly 5,400 cases in 2022 (source: lanigan-edwards.com/sauternes/c_coutet22.pdf; cf. sibling briefs 2018_p3_w3, 2018_p3_w4, 2022_p3_w9 for this producer's earlier vintages).
