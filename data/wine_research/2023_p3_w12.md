# Grenache, Yount Mill Vineyard, Newfound, 2019

## Quick tasting profile
A deliberately light, aromatic, cool-climate-styled Grenache from a warm-climate AVA: limpid, pale cherry-red colour, a nose of red fruits, berries, green/dried herbs and spice, and a savoury palate with brisk acidity animating strawberry and redcurrant fruit alongside grippy, fine tannin and a herbal, mineral edge from roughly 50% whole-cluster fermentation (source: https://www.vinography.com/2022/12/vinography-unboxed-week-of-12-18-22; https://jeroboamstrade.co.uk/product/2019-newfound-yount-mill-vineyard-grenache-napa-valley). At 13.0% ABV it reads markedly lighter and fresher than typical Napa reds or the two other Grenache/Garnacha wines in this flight — a strong stylistic signal of a modern, restrained, "anti-Napa" winemaking choice rather than a genuinely cool site.

## Technical specs
- Grape variety: Grenache (single varietal, single vineyard)
- Vineyard: Yount Mill Vineyard, a historic (farming since 1903) multi-block property in the Oakville/Yountville area of the Napa Valley floor, on gravelly, alluvial-fan soils derived from the Napa River; the vineyard has been certified organic since 1993 and is also California Sustainable Winegrowing Alliance certified (source: https://www.yountmillvineyards.com); Grenache occupies only a small fraction of the larger property (source: https://www.newfoundwines.com/vineyards)
- Soils: Gravel-rich alluvial soils on the valley floor (source: https://www.chamberswines.com/downloads/wines/marketing/12-11-2020-jancis-robinson-wine-of-the-week-newfound-gravels-grenache-2018-california.pdf)
- Vinification: Around 50% whole-cluster fermentation with native yeasts, gentle handling (foot-treading/pour-overs rather than aggressive extraction), roughly a month of skin contact (source: https://www.vinography.com/2022/12/vinography-unboxed-week-of-12-18-22; https://www.chamberswines.com/downloads/wines/marketing/12-11-2020-jancis-robinson-wine-of-the-week-newfound-gravels-grenache-2018-california.pdf)
- Elevage: Roughly a year in older/neutral vessels, unfined and unfiltered (source: https://www.chamberswines.com/downloads/wines/marketing/12-11-2020-jancis-robinson-wine-of-the-week-newfound-gravels-grenache-2018-california.pdf)
- ABV / RS / TA: 13.0% (exam-stated; consistent with the low-13% range reported for this label across vintages) / dry, not quantified / Source needed

## Producer style
Newfound Wines was founded in 2016 by Matt and Audra Naumann, who established a 40-acre estate in California's Sierra Foothills (El Dorado County) alongside sourcing from select Napa, Mendocino and other coastal vineyards; the winery later opened a tasting room in St Helena, Napa Valley (source: https://www.newfoundwines.com/about). The house philosophy is explicitly Mediterranean/Rhone-focused and minimal-intervention: organic/dry-farmed fruit where possible, native-yeast, whole-cluster fermentation, and neutral/large-format vessels that let site and variety show through rather than oak or extraction (source: https://www.newfoundwines.com/about). Newfound is part of a small but visible wave of Napa-adjacent producers using historic Rhone-variety sites (like Yount Mill) to make lighter, fresher, food-focused Grenache that deliberately departs from Napa's Cabernet-driven, riper commercial mainstream; the wine received notable critical attention, including a spot on a major US critic's Top 100 list for a related Newfound Grenache bottling (source: https://www.chamberswines.com/downloads/wines/marketing/12-11-2020-jancis-robinson-wine-of-the-week-newfound-gravels-grenache-2018-california.pdf).
