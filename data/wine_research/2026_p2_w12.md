# Château Rauzan-Ségla, 2016

## Quick tasting profile
A youthful but already refined Second Growth Margaux from an outstanding vintage: deep, dense purple-black colour with little sign of development at 9-10 years old, blackcurrant, violet, pink pepper and a mineral/graphite edge on the nose, and a medium-to-full-bodied, powerfully but finely structured palate — silky yet substantial tannin, real freshness, and a long, precise finish. This should taste noticeably more concentrated, structured and youthful than a 2017 from the same appellation.

## Technical specs
- Grape variety: 68% Cabernet Sauvignon, 30% Merlot, 2% Petit Verdot (official 2016 technical sheet)
- Vineyard: Estate parcels on the Margaux gravel plateau, Margaux commune
- Soils: Deep fine Günzian gravel over clay-limestone
- Vinification: Plot-by-plot, temperature-controlled fermentation with extended maceration typical of the estate (source needed for exact 2016 fermentation temperature/maceration length)
- Elevage: 18 months in French oak barriques, 60% new oak (remainder one-year-old barrels)
- ABV / RS / TA: 13.2–13.5% (sources vary slightly) / dry / Source needed
- Critic reception: James Suckling 97/100 and Wine Advocate 97/100 (blackcurrant, sweet tobacco, sandalwood/violet, silky but dense tannin, long polished finish); Decanter 95/100 (pristine black fruit, violets, truffle/cedar, fresh and seamless)
- Drinking window: estate and trade guidance suggest optimal drinking from roughly 2022 onward, with a peak around 2028-2038 and ageing potential into the 2045-2050 range — a substantially longer window than typically quoted for 2017 Margaux

## Vintage character (2016 Bordeaux / Margaux)
2016 is widely regarded as one of the outstanding modern Bordeaux vintages: a wet, cool winter/spring replenished water reserves, followed by a long, warm, largely dry summer and a cool September that preserved acidity while allowing full phenolic ripeness. Yields were moderate (not frost-reduced, unlike 2017) with strict selection at top estates, producing wines with the classic combination of ripe, well-integrated tannin, real freshness/acidity and serious structure for long-term ageing — critics across Decanter, Jancis Robinson and Wine Advocate rate 2016 as more powerful, more structurally complete and considerably longer-lived than the following, frost-affected 2017 vintage.

## Producer style
Château Rauzan-Ségla is a Second Growth (2ème Cru Classé, 1855) Margaux estate owned by the Wertheimer family (Chanel) since 1994, whose investment programme (new cellars, tighter selection, consultant input) drove a marked quality renaissance from the mid-1990s. House style emphasises precision, aromatic perfume and structural elegance from Margaux's gravel terroir without sacrificing depth or ageworthiness — widely cited as one of the appellation's most improved and currently most consistent classified growths.
