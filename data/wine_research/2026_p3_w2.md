# Cava Reserva Expression, Dominio de la Vega, 2022

## Quick tasting profile
Pale straw with fine, persistent bubbles from extended lees ageing. Nose shows baked/pastry autolytic notes (bollería) layered over fresh white fruit — apple, pear, citrus. Palate is round and well-structured with good acid grip, well-integrated carbonation and a creamy, lingering finish; this is a serious, autolysis-driven Cava rather than a simple fruity entry-level style (source: dominiodelavega.com product page, WebFetch extraction).

## Technical specs
- Grape variety: **100% Macabeo** (single-variety Reserva; the wider Dominio de la Vega range also uses Chardonnay, but this specific cuvée is confirmed monovarietal Macabeo) (source: dominiodelavega.com/productos/expression)
- Vineyard: La Vega estate, San Antonio de Requena, Utiel-Requena — high-altitude (~700-900m) sites on stony, chalk/clay soils; long cold winters, short warm summers give a cooler mesoclimate than coastal Penedès Cava (source: valenciawineconsulting.com; xpertvinum.be)
- Vinification: hand-harvested/selected fruit, traditional method (Método Tradicional) second fermentation in bottle
- Elevage: **minimum 18 months on lees** ("Crianza sobre lías mínimo 18 meses") — this satisfies (and exceeds the 9-month minimum for) the Cava Reserva category, consistent with the wine's stated Reserva classification (source: dominiodelavega.com)
- ABV / RS / TA: 12% / dosage not published by the producer — classified Brut, so RS falls within the 0-12 g/L Brut band; exact g/L figure is **Source needed**. TA not published.
- Malolactic / oak: not stated; no oak signature identified in any source — treated as none/neutral steel vinification.
- Farming: organic-certified estate (source: elracodelonostre.com; producer sustainability materials)

## Vintage character (2022 Utiel-Requena / Cava)

Utiel-Requena's high-altitude, continental mesoclimate (long cold winters, hot dry summers, wide diurnal range) generally supports high natural acidity in Macabeo despite the region's relatively southerly latitude — a structural point worth using in an origin/quality answer even without a year-specific report (source: valenciawineconsulting.com, general regional climate description, not 2022-specific).

## Producer style
Family winery founded 2001 near San Antonio de Requena, positioned as a quality-focused, sustainability-led outlier within Cava — Utiel-Requena sits well outside the Penedès heartland that produces the great majority of Cava, which is itself relevant to the "origin as closely as possible" mark. Dominio de la Vega emphasises organic certification, renewable energy and water conservation, and markets itself on terroir-driven, extended-lees-ageing Cava rather than commodity Brut. Reserva Especial bottling has scored 94/100 in blind competition per wineanorak's Cava overview, supporting a premium-tier quality read (source: wineanorak.com).

## Why it's in this exam
Wine 2 of the four-country sparkling flight (Q1, Paper 3). This wine specifically tests whether candidates can push origin beyond "Spain/Cava" to recognise a Cava that does NOT come from Penedès — Utiel-Requena is a legitimate but atypical Cava-producing zone, and the wine's structural seriousness (18 months lees, single-vineyard Macabeo, organic) argues against the default assumption that non-Penedès Cava is necessarily entry-level.
