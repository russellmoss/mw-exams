# Chardonnay Buttery Reserve, Josh Cellars, 2023

## Quick tasting profile
Deliberately styled, high-volume commercial Chardonnay: scents of juicy ripe stone fruit and baked lemon layered with vanilla, butter and caramel from oak/malolactic treatment, and flavors of ripe apple, luscious vanilla and toasted oak leading to a smooth, full-bodied finish. Overt buttery/creamy texture and sweet-oak signature dominate over site character (source: Josh Cellars / Total Wine retail copy, corroborated via multiple retailer listings).

## Technical specs
- Grape variety: Chardonnay (Central Coast, California — fruit "sourced from California's premier growing regions" per brand marketing; specific vineyard sites not published)
- Vinification: Explicitly marketed as using full malolactic fermentation plus oak barrel aging, with the stated winemaking goal of "matur[ing] Chardonnay in oak to transform tart flavors into softer, creamier ones" — i.e. this is a wine deliberately engineered toward a buttery, oak-forward commercial style rather than a site-transparent one (source: web search synthesis of Josh Cellars marketing copy)
- Oak regime: Described only generically as oak barrel aging in brand materials; specific % new oak, cooperage origin (American vs. French) and toast level are not published — Source needed for precise oak specification, though "toasted oak," "vanilla" and "caramel" descriptors are consistent with a significant new (likely American) oak component

- Retail price: approximately US$16-21 per 750ml, most commonly seen around $16.79-$20.99 (source: web search aggregation of Total Wine, Target, and other national retailers)

## Producer style
Josh Cellars was founded by Joseph Carr (named for his father) and has been owned/distributed by Deutsch Family Wine & Spirits since 2011. Under Deutsch's distribution muscle the brand scaled from roughly 1 million cases in 2016 to approximately 7.5 million cases by 2024 across 23 varietal SKUs, and is reported as the best-selling table wine under $20 at Total Wine & More, with continued double-digit growth even as the broader wine category has contracted. This is a large-scale, mass-market branded wine built on consistency, accessibility and a recognizable house style (vanilla/butter/caramel-forward Chardonnay) rather than vineyard specificity (source: web search synthesis of trade press on Deutsch Family Wine & Spirits and Josh Cellars brand growth).
