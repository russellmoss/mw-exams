# Château Delmond, 2023

## Quick tasting profile
Expect a pale-gold, unoaked botrytised Sauternes: acacia and white-floral lift over apricot, honey, peach and candied pineapple, with a citrus-peel/blood-orange freshness on the finish rather than a heavy, marmalade-toned wine. Medium-to-full body, refreshing acidity keeping the sweetness from feeling cloying, and a persistent but not over-extracted sweet finish — a lighter, fresher style than a top classed growth.

## Technical specs
- Grape variety: approx. 90% Sémillon, 10% Sauvignon Blanc (no Muscadelle reported) [1]
- Residual sugar: ~125 g/L (2018 fact-sheet figure; treated as representative of the estate's recent releases including 2022/2023/2025, as no vintage-specific 2023 figure was found — source needed for the exact 2023 number) [1]
- Vinification: harvested in multiple selective passes for botrytised berries (estate states at least 80% of the blend is botrytis-affected); pneumatic and vertical hydraulic pressing; alcoholic fermentation at 20-22°C [2][3]
- Elevage: 18 months in stainless steel tank, no oak; bottled with low free SO2 (~40 mg/L) [2][3]
- ABV / RS / TA: 13.5% / ~125 g/L (source: 2018 fact sheet, not vintage-specific) / source needed

## Vintage character (2023 Sauternes)
2023 is widely rated as one of the best recent Sauternes vintages (Wine Spectator 95, "best since 2014"; Wine Scholar Guild calls it part of a hat-trick of excellent vintages). Grapes reached full physiological and sugar ripeness before botrytis arrived; a storm around 21 September triggered rapid, widespread, high-quality noble rot after a warm, humid spell. Yields were low across the appellation (region average ~12-12.5 hl/ha, well below the ten-year mean) due to a mix of mildew pressure and rigorous sorting. Style is rich, concentrated and opulent but with fresher-than-usual acidity — top estates (Yquem 153 g/L RS, Suduiraut 150 g/L, Guiraud 130 g/L) recorded high sugars balanced by notable acid, and Decanter noted the usual Barsac/Sauternes flinty-vs-rich distinction was less pronounced than normal this year. [thewineindependent.com/articles/2023-sauternes; idealwine.info]

## Producer style
Château Delmond is the second Sauternes label of Jean-Christophe Barbe, a professor of oenology at Bordeaux University and a specialist in noble rot/botrytis chemistry (PhD thesis on SO2-binding in sweet wines), who also owns the flagship Château Laville. Delmond is sourced from younger vines in cooler parts of the vineyard and made deliberately unoaked and stainless-steel-aged to preserve freshness and purity, positioning it as an accessible, fresher-styled, earlier-drinking Sauternes rather than a long-aging classed-growth wine. Critical reception for 2023 was solid but mid-tier: La Revue du Vin de France 89/100 (demi-corps, acacia nose, candied pineapple/blood orange finish); Guide Hachette des Vins awarded two stars and a "coup de coeur." [larvf.com; wine-searcher.com]
