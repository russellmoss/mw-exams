# Gewürztraminer, Waimea Estates, 2014

## Quick tasting profile
Bright, pale straw-yellow with light green hues; intense aromas of rose petal, musk, and ginger with a minerally edge. Medium-dry on the palate with a concentrated core of rose petal and exotic florals interwoven with ginger and musk, smooth texture, and a finish of honey, spice, and exotic florals (source: Raymond Chan Wine Reviews, reviewed October 2014, 18.5/20). Fermented cool with no oak use, consistent with an aromatic, fruit-driven regional style rather than a textural/skin-contact approach.

## Technical specs
- Grape variety: 100% Gewürztraminer
- Vineyard sources: fruit drawn from parcels on the Waimea Plains and Ruby Bay, Nelson; estate-wide vineyard holdings exceed 140 hectares on stony, free-draining alluvial soils (source: raymondchanwinereviews.co.nz; waimeaestates.co.nz vineyard page)
- Vinification: cool fermentation; no oak used (source: raymondchanwinereviews.co.nz)
- ABV: 13.5%
- RS: 9.6 g/L (medium-dry / off-dry) (source: raymondchanwinereviews.co.nz)

## Producer style
Waimea Estates was founded in 1993 when Trevor and Robyn Bolitho planted vines on the stony alluvial soils of the Waimea Plains, Nelson; the estate's first commercial release (a 1997 Sauvignon Blanc) won Best Sauvignon Blanc at the Winestate Australasian Wine Awards (source: beveragetradenetwork.com). The Bolitho family owned and ran the estate until it was sold to Booster Tahi (a NZ KiwiSaver investment company) in late 2017 — meaning the 2014 vintage in question was made under family ownership, not the current corporate structure (source: toptastes.co.nz, cited within Tavily research synthesis; beveragetradenetwork.com for founding). The estate's core strength has historically been aromatic whites (Sauvignon Blanc, Gewürztraminer, Pinot Gris, Riesling) grown across 140+ hectares of Nelson vineyard.
